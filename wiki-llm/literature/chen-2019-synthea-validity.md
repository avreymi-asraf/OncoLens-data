# Chen et al., BMC Med Inform Decis Mak 19:44 (2019)

## One-Line Thesis
On four quality measures (incl. colorectal cancer screening, 63% compliant), Synthea's Massachusetts population diverged from real reported rates because Synthea models guideline-concordant care and "does not currently model deviations in care" or their outcomes.

## Reusable Ingredients
* Use Synthea only for code/pipeline tests, never as evidence about clinical signal. Target match: `G4`.

## Limitations
Old Synthea version; no developer rebuttal found.
