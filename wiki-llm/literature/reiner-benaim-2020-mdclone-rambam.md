# Reiner Benaim et al., JMIR Med Inform 8(2):e16492 (2020)

## One-Line Thesis
Across five observational studies at Rambam, results computed on MDClone synthetic derivatives were predictive of results on the real data; repeated synthetic draws estimate bias and stability.

## Reusable Ingredients
* Workflow: build cohort → analyse synthetic → validate on real inside the hospital. A governance pattern Hadassah could follow.
* Target match: `G4` (synthetic-to-real transfer for *predictive models* is untested — open question).

## Limitations
Observational statistics only; no ML/prediction tasks; vendor co-involvement.
