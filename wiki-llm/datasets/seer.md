# SEER

US population registry: stage, histology, first-course treatment, survival. **Access change: since 13 June 2025 anyone with a valid email can access SEER Research Data**; no restrictions by email provider or "countries of concern". Research Plus needs eRA Commons. **Use:** survival baselines and priors by stage/histology; external sanity check of Israeli survival curves. Not an EMR.
