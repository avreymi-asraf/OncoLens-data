# Flatiron Health

Gold-standard commercial oncology RWD (technology-enabled abstraction; real-world PFS/OS definitions). Academic access via institutional programs with fees; international TRE (Lifebit) for UK/Germany/Japan. **Unlikely to be accessible** to HUJI without a sponsor. Its **endpoint definitions** (rwPFS, real-world response) are worth adopting as label definitions even without the data.
