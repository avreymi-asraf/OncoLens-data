# Clalit Research Institute & Samueli Institute cancer data lake

## What it is
Clalit is Israel's largest HMO and owns hospitals (incl. Beilinson/Rabin). The Samueli Integrative Cancer Pioneering Institute at Beilinson was set up with access to Clalit's cancer patient data as a "data lake". Data-availability statements in Clalit papers: access is subject to internal review, a data-sharing agreement and Clalit IRB approval, inside a secure setting, case by case. Commercial channels exist (2012 XTL deal; Lynx MD partnership for anonymized data).

## Why it matters
The most Hadassah-like data in Israel (same language, same national coding, overlapping drugs and guidelines) plus **community follow-up** that a hospital EMR lacks. Strongest candidate for an **Israeli external validation** cohort, but requires a collaboration.
