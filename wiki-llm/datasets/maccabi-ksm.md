# Maccabi KSM

## What it is
Maccabi's research center: 2.7M members, 30 years of data, an online research platform, a data-partnership framework, IRB approval support. Oncology publications include palbociclib and breast-cancer survival (2023), cancer-associated VTE, and a short-term breast-cancer risk model built on ~1M records. Maccabi partnered with MDClone in 2017 ([[datasets/mdclone-israel]]).

## Why it matters
Community-side treatment and outcomes (drug dispensing, labs, survival). Weaker on in-hospital oncology detail. A possible second Israeli cohort for outcomes that do not depend on hospital notes.
